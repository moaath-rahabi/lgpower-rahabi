# Changelog

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.40.1 - Community Edition] - 2026-11-01

### Official Branding & Identity
- **Official Branding**: Integrated high-resolution emblem and wordmark designed for Rahabi LG Power.
- **Adaptive Launcher Icon**: Custom adaptive emblem icon with safe zone margins and high-contrast dark emerald background (`#0D1411`) supporting circle, squircle, and rounded-corner launcher masks.
- **Professional About Page**: Dedicated in-app About screen displaying official branding, Community Edition badge, developer attribution (**Eng. Moaath Rahabi**), upstream open-source credits (**LG Power by Nicsilver**), AGPL-3.0 license viewer, and third-party notices.
- **Header Branding**: Branded header emblem retaining authentic colors regardless of active application theme.

### Advanced TV Channel Management
- **Full TV Channel Synchronization**: Fast channel fetching and persistent caching supporting large channel databases (870+ channels).
- **Interactive Channel Search**: Instant real-time filtering by channel name, broadcast callsign, or channel number.
- **Personalized Channel Groups**: Create, rename, delete, and filter channels by custom user categories (e.g. Sports, News, Movies).
- **Favorites System**: Quick-toggle favorite channels with dedicated "Favorites" tab and instant access.
- **Channel Customization**: Override TV broadcast names, numbers, and manage multi-digit jumping.
- **Non-Destructive Reset**: Per-channel customization reset and global reset to restore original TV broadcast configurations.

### Ergonomic Right-Handed Remote UX
- **Right-Handed Thumb Reach**: Restructured control surface placing volume down/up, channel up/down, and quick playback controls within comfortable right-handed thumb range.
- **Full-Width Control Surfaces**: Expanded D-pad, smooth Trackpad, and high-density Numpad to occupy full content width without side cramping.
- **Continuous Gesture Controls**: Horizontal volume scrubber with instant step/drag response and vertical channel swipe trackpad.
- **Quick Controls Card**: Added dedicated tiles for USB Media Player, Guide, Screen Off, Quick Menu, Picture Mode, Sound EQ, and Subtitles.
- **Quick Apps Rail**: Configurable direct-launch bar for favorite TV streaming apps.

### Unified Theming System & Accessibility
- **Full Dynamic Theming**: Theme engine controlling all activities (`MainActivity`, `ChannelsActivity`, `SettingsActivity`, `AboutActivity`).
- **Authentic Theme Presets**: Rahabi Charcoal Gold, Dracula, Nord, Catppuccin, Monokai, Light, Dark, One Light, and Solarized Light.
- **Contrast & Legibility Fixes**: Elevated secondary labels and icons to meet WCAG AA contrast standards across all dark and polar themes.

## [1.40.0] - Upstream Baseline - 2026-10-26

- Touchpad mode option swappable with D-pad.

- Keyboard probe and direct TV search fallback.
