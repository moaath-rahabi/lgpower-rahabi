## Community Modifications

The modifications and enhancements in this repository were developed by:
**Eng. Moaath Rahabi** (2026)

### Key Modifications:
1. **Advanced TV Channel Management:** Channel synchronization, live channel search, custom user channel groups, favorites management, and non-destructive channel resets.
2. **Ergonomic Right-Handed Remote UX:** Restructured control surface placing volume down/up, channel up/down, and playback controls within comfortable right-handed thumb reach.
3. **Full-Width Control Surfaces:** Expanded D-pad, smooth Trackpad, and high-density Numpad to occupy full content width without side cramping.
4. **Continuous Gesture Controls:** Horizontal volume scrubber and vertical channel swipe trackpad.
5. **Quick Access:** Dedicated tiles for USB Media Player, Guide, Screen Off, Quick Menu, Picture Mode, Sound EQ, and Subtitles.
6. **Unified Theming Engine:** Dynamic theme engine controlling all application screens with 9 authentic themes (Rahabi Charcoal Gold, Nord, Dracula, Catppuccin, Monokai, Light, Dark, One Light, Solarized Light).
7. **Official Branding & Identity:** Official logo integration, adaptive launcher icon, and dedicated in-app About page.
